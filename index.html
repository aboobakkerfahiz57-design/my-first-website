About TBD GROUP.in

Growing with vision, trust and quality.

Three Visions. One Mission.

TBD GROUP.in is a growing business group based in Mangaluru, Karnataka, bringing together construction, real estate and interior design under one vision.

Our focus is on creating reliable property opportunities, quality construction, premium interiors and long-term value for our customers.

We believe that every project should be built on trust, quality and commitment.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and premium business group from coastal Karnataka, serving customers through real estate, construction and interior design.


Our Mission

Deliver quality spaces and property solutions while building strong, long-term relationships with our customers.

Meet the Founder

Leadership behind TBD GROUP.in

ABOOBAKKER FAHIZ

FOUNDER & CHAIRMAN

The Founder and Chairman of TBD Group Pvt Ltd Company in Mangaluru is Aboobakker Fahiz.

He leads the company's multi-wing operations, overseeing structural execution under TBD Builders & Developers, premium property transactions under SR PRIME Properties, and interior conceptualization through AURELIV Interiors Design.

Alongside his real estate development ventures, Aboobakker Fahiz has been involved in youth and student leadership activities in the Dakshina Kannada region.

Under his leadership, TBD GROUP.in operates as a growing infrastructure and property startup in coastal Karnataka.
